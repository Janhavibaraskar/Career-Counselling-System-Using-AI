About the Platform

Intelligent Career Guidance Powered by AI

A next-generation career counseling platform that helps students make confident academic and career decisions using structured assessments and intelligent analysis.

The AI Career Counseling & Skill Gap Analysis System is built for students from Class 10, Class 12, and Graduation. It evaluates academic background, interests, and responses from an adaptive assessment to deliver personalized, transparent, and data-driven recommendations.

  • ✔ Stream and career recommendations based on aptitude
  • ✔ Adaptive assessment covering logic, behavior, and personality
  • ✔ Clear scorecards highlighting strengths and skill gaps

Our aim is to bridge the gap between student potential and the right career opportunities through intelligent decision support.
